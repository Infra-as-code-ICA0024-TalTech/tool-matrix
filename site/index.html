About the criteria and tool inventory

The slide supplies the labels and scores but no scoring rubric, version list, or assessment date. The labels are retained without assigning new definitions or guessing additional scores. “SM index” is the title used on the source image.

The catalog includes infrastructure automation, workflow tools, and runtimes explicitly used or documented in demos 01–06, including the 03 and 04 variants. Kubernetes is retained from the source slide. Deployed application services and individual operating-system utilities are outside this automation-tool inventory. Optional tools are labeled in their evidence notes.
